# Client onboarding

## Trigger

Begin onboarding only after the approved agreement and required payment condition are satisfied. These triggers are blocked until founder decisions are complete.

## Checklist

- Confirm legal client name, primary contact, decision-maker, and working team.
- Restate scope, exclusions, communication channels, meeting cadence, and change process.
- Create private client workspace and access register.
- Request only required access; prefer role-based access over shared passwords.
- Confirm data handling, recording, approval, and publication permissions.
- Schedule kickoff and evidence deadlines.
- Define baseline funnel terms and owners.
- Identify known seasonality, operational capacity, market events, and constraints.
- Confirm who approves copy, technical changes, spend, and public claims.
- Document escalation and offboarding procedures.

Never store credentials or client-sensitive exports in this repository.
