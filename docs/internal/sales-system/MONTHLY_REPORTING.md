# Monthly reporting

## Objective

Support decisions and accountability. Do not use reporting to inflate activity or hide weak evidence.

## Recommended structure

1. Executive summary: what changed and why it matters
2. Business context: seasonality, capacity, market events, operational changes
3. Funnel performance using agreed definitions
4. Work completed and quality verification
5. Experiments: hypothesis, change, evidence, learning, next decision
6. Risks, blockers, missing data, and client actions
7. Priorities for the next period

## Metric discipline

Show source, period, comparison method, definition, and confidence. Separate leading indicators from business outcomes. Never imply causality from a correlation or short observation window.

## Meeting outcome

End with documented decisions, owners, deadlines, and what Lumina recommends stopping or deferring.
