# Diagnostic workflow

## Intake

1. Confirm approved scope, evidence access, participants, and handling of sensitive data.
2. Create a private client workspace outside this repository.
3. Record framework version and audit period.
4. Define client terms: inquiry, qualified lead, appointment/estimate, customer/job, and revenue attribution.

## Evidence collection

- Business and market context
- Google Business Profile and local visibility
- Website, service/location architecture, forms, and calls
- Review/reputation workflow
- Lead routing, response, follow-up, and pipeline
- Analytics, call tracking, CRM/spreadsheets, and baseline records
- Technology inventory and automation candidates

Record the source, owner, period, access limit, and reliability of each item.

## Analysis

1. Complete relevant category observations.
2. Separate facts, client statements, Lumina inference, and unknowns.
3. Assign confidence; use exploratory maturity only when useful.
4. Identify cross-category constraints rather than producing eleven isolated checklists.
5. Draft recommendations by impact, confidence, effort, dependency, owner, and timing.
6. Identify what should not be prioritized.

## Quality review and presentation

Use the checklist in `docs/audit-framework/README.md`. Present the reasoning and tradeoffs, invite corrections, and log decisions. The audit itself does not authorize implementation.
