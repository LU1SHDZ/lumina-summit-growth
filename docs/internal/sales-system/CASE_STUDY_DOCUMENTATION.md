# Case-study documentation

## Permission and integrity

Obtain explicit written approval for the client name, logo, images, quotations, metrics, and distribution channels. An agreement to work together is not publication permission.

## Evidence record

- Original business context and constraints
- Baseline definitions, period, sources, and confidence
- Approved scope and client responsibilities
- What changed and when
- External factors, seasonality, and concurrent initiatives
- Outcomes observed and evidence source
- What cannot be attributed confidently
- Client-approved quotation, if any

## Narrative structure

1. Context
2. Constraint
3. Evidence and reasoning
4. Intervention
5. Collaboration
6. Observed outcome
7. Limitations and learning
8. Next step

Never convert an unmeasured prototype, design concept, or client statement into a performance claim. Preserve a redacted evidence packet in the approved private client system.
