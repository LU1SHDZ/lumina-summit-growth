# Proposal workflow

## Prerequisites

- Mutual fit has been confirmed.
- The problem and desired decision are understood.
- Scope, dependencies, client responsibilities, and exclusions are known.
- Pricing, duration, contract, and risk-reversal decisions are founder-approved.

## Proposal structure

1. Client context and understood constraint
2. Desired outcome stated without guarantee
3. Recommended engagement and rationale
4. Scope and tangible deliverables
5. Client inputs and access required
6. Milestones/timing when approved
7. Measurement approach and baseline limitations
8. Explicit exclusions
9. Investment/payment terms when approved
10. Assumptions, change process, and expiration
11. Agreement/signature process

## Review

Check every claim against discovery evidence. Do not insert placeholder commercial terms into a proposal. Record proposal sent, revisions, decision date, loss reason, and final outcome only in the approved system of record.
