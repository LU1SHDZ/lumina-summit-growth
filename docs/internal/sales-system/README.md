# Sales and delivery operating system

Working lifecycle:

1. Growth Snapshot request received
2. Human qualification review
3. Discovery call
4. Growth Snapshot review
5. Growth Blueprint diagnostic when appropriate
6. Foundation or Website proposal and agreement
7. Client onboarding
8. Baseline confirmation
9. Focused implementation
10. 30/60/90-day roadmap
11. Optional Local Growth or Growth Partner engagement
12. Measurement and reporting
13. Case-study/referral review when appropriate

These files are operating drafts, not approved legal agreements or promises. Founder decisions in `FOUNDER_DECISIONS.md` take precedence where a commercial choice is unresolved.
