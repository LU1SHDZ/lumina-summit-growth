# 30/60/90-day planning template

The labels below describe sequencing after the Sprint; they are not a promise that every recommendation fits these exact windows.

## First 30 days — Stabilize and observe

- Correct critical trust, routing, tracking, or conversion failures.
- Establish owners and agreed definitions.
- Capture a usable baseline.
- Avoid scaling spend into a broken path.

## Days 31–60 — Build the priority foundation

- Implement the highest-confidence improvements whose dependencies are ready.
- Document changes and QA across devices/channels.
- Train owners and establish exception handling.
- Begin comparison against the baseline without premature conclusions.

## Days 61–90 — Learn and improve

- Review evidence quality and early directional outcomes.
- Test a limited hypothesis with an explicit success condition.
- Improve the process, not only the creative/channel.
- Decide what to continue, stop, change, or defer.

Every item should have an owner, rationale, dependency, evidence target, review date, and status.
