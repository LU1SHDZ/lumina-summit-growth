# Discovery call workflow

## Objective

Determine whether a useful problem, mutual fit, readiness, and responsible next step exist. The call is not a disguised audit or pressure presentation.

## Before the call

- Review the complete application and public business presence.
- Identify missing context; do not pre-diagnose from the website alone.
- Record the acquisition source and application timestamp.
- Confirm attendees and decision roles.
- Prepare 3–5 questions specific to the stated constraint.

## Suggested agenda

1. Set expectations and available time.
2. Ask what prompted the conversation now.
3. Understand services, markets, customers, economics, and capacity at a useful level.
4. Trace the current journey from discovery to won/lost work.
5. Understand what has been tried, what evidence exists, and what feels uncertain.
6. Clarify decision process, urgency, access, and internal ownership.
7. Reflect the problem back; invite correction.
8. Recommend one of: no action, client-led next step, deeper diagnostic/Sprint, or another resource.

## Required notes

- Stated problem and desired outcome
- Business/market context
- Current acquisition and follow-up system
- Evidence available and unavailable
- Decision roles and timing
- Risks, poor-fit indicators, and conflicts
- Recommended next step and why

## Exit criteria

Do not advance unless Lumina can explain the likely value of a diagnostic without promising an outcome. Record “not a fit” honestly and include any useful referral or client-led action.
